# Release deleted usernames and emails after 7 days

MyTuums is handle-first, but v1 releases usernames and emails 7 days after account deletion. This trades permanent or long handle reservation and stronger impersonation protection for a smaller reserved namespace, simpler user-facing retention rules, and easier fresh-start reuse; the delay gives a short safety buffer before a deleted handle or email can be claimed again. After reuse, `/@{username}` resolves to the current holder only; historical audit, moderation, and notification references use internal IDs and must not rebind deleted-user history to a new account.
