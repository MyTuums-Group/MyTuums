# Launch public media uploads without automated scanning in v1

MyTuums v1 allows public image and video uploads but does not include automated malware, CSAM, nudity, or policy scanning. This accepts a launch risk to keep the MVP limited to direct blob uploads, reactive reports, rate limits, clear Terms, and fast staff removal; adding scanning would introduce vendor choice, privacy handling, false-positive workflows, and retroactive moderation concerns outside the v1 scope.
